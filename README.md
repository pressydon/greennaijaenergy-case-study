# GreenNaijaEnergy
### Energy Management & Green Energy Adoption

**A product initiative by Precious Dim** · [Live website](https://greennaijaenergy.com)

GreenNaijaEnergy is an energy-management web application built around a practical challenge: helping people in Nigeria make better decisions about how they manage power and explore greener energy options.

The long-term vision is to make energy information and cleaner-energy choices easier to understand, with the possibility of adapting the product for other markets over time.

## The problem space

Energy choices can be difficult when people have limited visibility into their power use and the options available to them. Better energy-management experiences can help users think more clearly about consumption, cost, and alternatives.

GreenNaijaEnergy is being developed with this problem in mind, with a broader ambition to support green-energy adoption.

## Product direction

- **Energy management:** build a digital experience focused on helping users manage power.
- **Greener alternatives:** support the broader goal of helping Nigerians explore cleaner energy options.
- **Room to scale:** shape the product with the potential to serve users beyond Nigeria.

These points describe the product direction and ambition; they should not be read as a claim that every capability is already implemented or commercially launched.

## My contribution
